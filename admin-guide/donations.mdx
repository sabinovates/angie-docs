Set up donation campaigns and see what's been given, by whom.

![Admin donations page](/images/admin/donations.png)

Create a campaign with a name and, optionally, a goal amount. Once it's live, anyone can give from your association's public **Donate** page — donors don't need to be logged-in members. You can also record an offline donation (cash, bank transfer) directly from this page.

Export donation records to CSV for your own bookkeeping.
