---
title: "Audit log"
description: "A searchable record of every admin action taken on your association"
---

Every meaningful action an admin takes — approving a member, changing a setting, refunding a payment — is recorded here, with who did it and when.

![Admin audit log page](/images/admin/audit-log.png)

Search and paginate through the log, and export it to CSV if you need it for compliance or dispute resolution.
