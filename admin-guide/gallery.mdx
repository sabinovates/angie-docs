---
title: "Gallery"
description: "Publish photo albums for members to browse"
---

Manage the photo albums members see in their own [gallery](/member-guide/gallery) view.

![Admin gallery page](/images/admin/gallery.png)

Create an album, upload photos, and set its status (draft, published, or archived) — an album only appears to members once published. Reorder albums using the **Reorder** control to control the order they appear in.
