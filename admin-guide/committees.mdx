---
title: "Committees"
description: "Set up committees and their members, separate from groups"
---

Committees are a separate structure from [groups](/admin-guide/groups) — meant for working committees (planning, finance, welfare) rather than chapters or class sets.

![Admin committees page](/images/admin/committees.png)

Create a committee, then add members to it with a specific role on that committee. A member already on a committee won't show up again in the "add member" picker, so you can't double-add the same person by mistake.
