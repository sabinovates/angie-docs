The Members page is the full roster — every approved member, searchable and filterable.

![Admin members page](/images/admin/members.png)

## Adding members

- **One at a time** — select **Add member** and fill in their details directly.
- **In bulk** — use **Import**, which takes a CSV. Download the template first so your columns match what Angie expects. Importing previews every row before committing, flags rows it can't import, and gives you a skip report afterward — an import either fully succeeds or fully rolls back, so you never end up with half a file applied.

## Managing existing members

- Select any member to open their detail page, where you can edit their record, suspend them, or remove them entirely.
- Use **bulk assign group** or **bulk change status** to act on several selected members at once.
- **Export** to CSV whenever you need the roster outside Angie.

<Warning>
  Removing a member deletes their account. Suspending is reversible and is almost always the better choice if you're unsure.
</Warning>
