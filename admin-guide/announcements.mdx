---
title: "Announcements"
description: "Compose and publish official posts to your members"
---

Announcements are how you broadcast news to members — shown on their [announcements page](/member-guide/announcements) and, depending on your settings, sent by email, SMS, or WhatsApp too.

![Admin announcements page](/images/admin/announcements.png)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Compose">
    Select **New announcement** and write it using the rich-text editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview your audience">
    Before publishing, preview exactly who will receive it — all members, a specific group, or a membership type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set an expiry (optional)">
    Give it an expiry date if it should stop showing to members after a certain point.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Save and publish. You can edit or delete it later from the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>
