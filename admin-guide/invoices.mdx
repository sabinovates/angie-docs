---
title: "Invoices & receipts"
description: "Create multi-line invoices and send payment reminders"
---

Invoices give you more control than a simple fee assignment — useful when you're billing a member for several line items at once.

![Admin invoices & receipts page](/images/admin/invoices.png)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an invoice">
    Select **Add invoice**, pick the member, and add as many line items as you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record a payment">
    When the member pays (even outside the platform, like a bank transfer), record it against the invoice from its detail view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a reminder">
    Use **Remind** on a single invoice, or **Bulk remind** to nudge everyone with an outstanding balance at once. Pick which channel (email, SMS, WhatsApp) to send it through.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Download a receipt as a PDF from any paid invoice's detail view.
