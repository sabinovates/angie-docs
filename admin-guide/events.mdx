---
title: "Events"
description: "Create and manage events, from draft to published"
---

Create events here — reunions, meetings, fundraisers — and control whether members can see and register for them.

![Admin events page](/images/admin/events.png)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an event">
    Select **Add event** and fill in the date, location, description, and any registration requirements or fees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish it">
    A new event starts as a draft, invisible to members. Select **Publish** when it's ready to go live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track registrations and check-in">
    Once published, see who's registered from the event's detail view, and use [Attendance](/admin-guide/attendance) to check people in on the day.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Cancelling an event keeps its record but notifies anyone already registered.
