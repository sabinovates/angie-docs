---
title: "Attendance"
description: "Check members in at an event using their ticket QR code"
---

Attendance tracks who actually showed up to an event, separate from who registered.

![Admin attendance page](/images/admin/attendance.png)

Open an event's attendance view and either scan each attendee's ticket QR code (from a phone or a scanner) or mark them present manually from the list. You can also unmark someone if you check them in by mistake.
