---
title: "Finance overview"
description: "A top-level view of your association's money"
---

The Finance overview rolls up dues, donations, and payments into one picture: how much is coming in, how much is outstanding, and how it's trending.

![Admin finance overview](/images/admin/finance.png)

From here you can drill into [Dues](/admin-guide/dues), [Donations](/admin-guide/donations), [Projects](/admin-guide/projects), [Payments](/admin-guide/payments), and [Invoices & receipts](/admin-guide/invoices) for the detail behind each number. Export the whole picture to CSV from the **Export** button.
