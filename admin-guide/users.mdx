---
title: "Users"
description: "Add the people who help run your association, and set what each one can do"
---

Users here means your admin team — the people who log into the admin console, as opposed to ordinary members.

![Admin users page](/images/admin/users.png)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a user">
    Select **Add user**, enter their details, and assign a role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change their role">
    Update a user's role at any time from the list — see [Roles & permissions](/admin-guide/roles) for what each role can do.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset a password or disable access">
    Trigger a password reset for a user who's locked out, or disable their access entirely if they've left the team.
  </Step>
</Steps>
