---
title: "Communication log"
description: "A record of every email, SMS, and WhatsApp message sent to members"
---

Every message your association has sent — announcements, reminders, invoice nudges — is logged here, along with delivery status.

![Admin communication log](/images/admin/communication-log.png)

Use this when a member says they didn't get something: you can confirm whether it was sent, and whether it was delivered, opened, or clicked (for email).
