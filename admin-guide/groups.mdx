---
title: "Groups"
description: "Organize members into groups, such as chapters or class sets"
---

Groups let you organize members — by graduating class, chapter, or however your association structures itself.

![Admin groups page](/images/admin/groups.png)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a group">
    Select **Add group**, name it, and save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a leader">
    Open the group and search for a member to set as group leader using the typeahead search.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage membership">
    Add or remove members from the group's detail page. Members can also request to join or leave a group themselves from their own [settings](/member-guide/settings) — those requests land with you for approval.
  </Step>
</Steps>
