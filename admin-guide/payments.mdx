---
title: "Payments"
description: "Every payment made to your association, in one master-detail view"
---

Every payment — dues, donations, event fees, wallet deposits — lands here, regardless of which feature it came from.

![Admin payments page](/images/admin/payments.png)

Select any payment in the list to see its full detail on the right: who paid, how, when, and its current status. If a payment needs to be reversed, select **Refund** from its detail view.

Export the whole list to CSV from the **Export** button.
