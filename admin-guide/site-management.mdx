---
title: "Site management"
description: "Manage your association's public-facing site content"
---

Site management is a lightweight CMS for your association's public pages — the "About" page, leadership listing, and other public-site content, separate from the admin console itself.

![Admin site management page](/images/admin/site-management.png)

Each tab covers a different part of the public site. The leadership tab is where you add or remove the people shown on your public "About" or "Leadership" page — separate from the [Users](/admin-guide/users) who actually log into the admin console.
