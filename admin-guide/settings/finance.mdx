---
title: "Finance settings"
description: "Bank accounts members pay dues into, and other finance configuration"
---

Set up the bank account(s) your association collects dues and donations into, separate from any individual member's own saved bank accounts.

![Admin finance settings](/images/admin/settings-finance.png)

Add or remove association bank accounts here. These show up to members as payment options where relevant, such as manual bank-transfer instructions.
