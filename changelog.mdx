A plain-language look at what's changed recently, newest first. For the full technical log, see the project's `CHANGELOG.md`.

## October 20, 2026

- The marketing website's menu now works properly on phones and tablets, with a slide-out menu replacing navigation links that previously disappeared on small screens.
- The member portal now works properly on phones: a slide-out menu replaces the sidebar, wide tables (wallet, events, dues, invoices, payments, directory) show as tap-friendly cards instead of forcing a horizontal scroll, and buttons are sized for touch.
- Every association's public site (home, about, events, gallery, donate, contact) is now fully responsive on phones and tablets, including a working mobile menu that was previously unresponsive.
- The association login and registration pages are now mobile-friendly, showing a compact header instead of the full branding panel on small screens so the form takes center stage.
- Fixed the custom domain (useangie.com) not resolving association subdomains in production.

## October 17, 2026

- Added a branded "page expired" screen, shown when a form session times out, matching the look of the existing not-found and maintenance pages.

## October 16, 2026

- Rebuilt the Gallery for both admins and members. Admins can now create and manage real photo categories (add, rename, delete), set a cover photo per album, and remove individual photos. The member portal's Gallery page — previously a placeholder — now shows published albums with a full-screen photo viewer.
- Subscription upgrades paid through Paystack are now billed in Naira (NGN) instead of US dollars.
- Admins viewing their own member portal can now jump straight to the admin console with a new "Admin Portal" button next to the notification bell.
- This documentation site went live.

## October 15, 2026

- Members now get an email receipt for every wallet deposit, withdrawal request, and transfer, and a confirmation whenever a bank account is added or removed.
- Both association admins and members now get a confirmation email whenever they change their password or turn two-factor authentication on or off.
- Association admins can now change their own password from Settings, without needing another admin to reset it for them.
- Association admins now get an email whenever their network is suspended or reactivated, and whenever a subscription request is submitted, approved, rejected, or changed.
- Fixed a bug where the marketing homepage could appear blank until the page was scrolled.
- Fixed a bug where a missing real-time chat configuration could silently break other parts of the page.
- Angie went live on its production infrastructure for the first time.

## October 14, 2026

- Added member-count-based pricing tiers with real enforcement — your plan now actually limits how many members you can approve.
- Added usage warnings at 80%, 90%, 100%, and over your plan's member limit.
- Association admins can now request a plan upgrade or downgrade, reviewed by Angie before it takes effect.
- Added an interactive pricing calculator, linked from the pricing page.
- The marketing pricing page and the signup wizard's plan step now show live, real pricing instead of placeholder numbers.
- Fixed a couple of database issues that could interrupt setup on some installations.

## October 13, 2026

- Switched to a new email delivery provider for outgoing mail.
- Fixed inconsistent spacing in report filter rows, and reports now load a page at a time instead of all at once.

## October 12, 2026

- Added member wallets — deposit, withdraw, and send money to other members.
- Members can now save their own bank accounts for wallet withdrawals.
- Added branded error pages instead of generic ones.
- Association admins can now turn two-factor authentication on or off for their own account independently.
- Fixed the admin dashboard showing outdated member counts.

## October 11, 2026

- Renamed "Contact Angie" to "Angie Support System."
- Refreshed the look of most of the member portal: dashboard, payment history, profile, membership card, directory, events, dues & fees, donations, invoices & receipts, announcements, meetings, and notifications.
- Added search and pagination to the admin audit log.
- Fixed the member dashboard missing a photo slot, and fixed membership expiry dates not being set automatically when a member was approved.

## October 9, 2026

- Added dedicated admin pages for Projects and Committees.
- Refreshed the look of admin finance and communication pages: overview, dues & fees, donations, payments, invoices & receipts (now with multi-line invoices), announcements, and meetings.
- Admins can now edit and delete announcements after publishing.

## October 8, 2026

- Refreshed the look of admin Groups, Membership types, Events, Attendance, and Gallery pages, each with real working functionality behind them.
- Member import and export now has a real, working backend.

## October 3, 2026

- Added two-way support messaging between members and Angie's team, delivered in real time.
- Added real, configurable admin roles with granular permissions.
- Site management rebuilt as a proper content management tool for your public pages.
- Refreshed the look of Finance settings, Audit log, Users & Roles, admin Dashboard, Reports, All Members, and Applications.

## October 2, 2026

- Added CSV member import, self-service group join/leave requests, calendar export for events, a rich-text editor for announcements, downloadable PDF membership cards, membership renewal reminders, and QR-code event check-in.
- Added email and SMS/WhatsApp delivery for notifications, with delivery tracking.
- Replaced old-style browser pop-up confirmations with proper on-screen dialogs throughout the app.
- Refreshed the look of the member dashboard and split community settings into clearer, separate pages.

## October 1, 2026

- Added the admin finance, communications, and administration sections.
- Added a complete set of branded transactional emails.
- Added two-factor authentication for members and admins.
- Added real photo and file uploads for logos, covers, and avatars.
- Added CSV export to every list page.
- Added account lockout after repeated failed login attempts.
- Improved mobile layout and accessibility across the app.

## Late September 2026

- Angie's foundation went in: multi-tenant association sites, the internal operations console, the association signup and application pipeline, member registration, the member portal, and the admin console's membership, events, and attendance tools.
