---
title: "First login & inviting your team"
description: "Log in for the first time and add other admins"
---

Once your association is approved, you'll get an email with your site's address and your first admin login.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your site">
    Your association lives at its own subdomain, like
    `yourassociation.useangie.com`. If you don't know it, the marketing site's
    **Log in** page can look it up by your association's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Use the email and temporary password from your approval email. You'll be
    prompted to set a new password on first login.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up two-factor authentication">
    From **Settings → Security**, turn on two-factor authentication for your own
    account. If the platform requires 2FA, you'll be walked through this
    automatically before you can continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    From **Settings → Administration → Users**, add the other people who'll help
    run the association and assign each one a role. See [Users &
    roles](/admin-guide/users) for what each role can do.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Keep your admin login private. Anyone with admin access can see member data,
  process payments, and send communications on your association's behalf.
</Warning>
