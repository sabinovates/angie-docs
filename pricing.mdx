Angie prices by member count, not by feature access. Every plan includes the full platform — membership management, finance, events, communication, and reporting. As your association grows past a tier's limit, you move up; if it shrinks, you can move back down.

![Angie marketing homepage, with pricing tiers](/images/marketing/home.png)

## Tiers

Tiers scale from small associations just getting started up through large, established ones with over a thousand members. Exact pricing and tier boundaries are shown live on the marketing site's pricing section, since they're configured centrally and may be adjusted over time — this page won't repeat numbers that could drift out of date.

Prices are shown in USD by default, with a toggle to view in NGN.

## Pricing calculator

Use the interactive calculator to estimate your cost by sliding your expected member count.

![Pricing calculator](/images/marketing/pricing-calculator.png)

<Tip>
  The calculator is for estimating — your actual plan is confirmed during [signup](/getting-started/signing-up) or when you [change plans](/getting-started/choosing-a-plan) later.
</Tip>
