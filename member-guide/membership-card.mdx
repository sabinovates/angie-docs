Every member has a digital membership card showing your name, membership number, photo, and expiry date (if your membership type has one).

![Membership card](/images/member/card.png)

## Downloading a PDF copy

Select **Download PDF** on the membership card page to save a printable copy.

## The QR code

Each card carries a QR code that anyone can scan to verify your membership is genuine and current — useful for discounts or events run by partner organizations that want to confirm you're an active member.
