## Dues

The Dues page lists every fee assigned to you — annual dues, one-off levies, anything your association has billed you for — and whether each one is paid, pending, or overdue.

![Member dues](/images/member/dues.png)

- Pay a single due by selecting it, or use **Pay all** to clear everything outstanding in one payment.
- Paying through your [wallet](/member-guide/wallet) balance or a card both work.

## Payment history

Every payment you've made — dues, donations, wallet deposits — shows up here, searchable by reference, description, date, amount, method, or status.

![Member payment history](/images/member/payments.png)

<Tip>
  If a payment shows as declined, a banner appears at the top of this page so you don't have to go looking for it. Select **Retry** to try again.
</Tip>

Export your payment history to CSV from the **Export** button if you need records for your own tracking.
