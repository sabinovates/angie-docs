The dashboard is what you see right after logging in. It gives you a quick read on your account: outstanding dues, upcoming events, recent announcements, and your membership status at a glance.

![Member dashboard](/images/member/dashboard.png)

## What's on it

- **Membership status** — your current standing (active, pending renewal, etc.) and expiry date, if your membership type has one.
- **Outstanding dues** — a shortcut straight to [Dues & payments](/member-guide/dues-and-payments) if you owe anything.
- **Upcoming events** — the next events you can register for, linking to [Events](/member-guide/events).
- **Recent announcements** — the latest posts from your association, linking to [Announcements](/member-guide/announcements).

<Tip>
  Everything on the dashboard links somewhere — treat it as a shortcut list, not a separate feature.
</Tip>
