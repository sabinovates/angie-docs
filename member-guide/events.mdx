---
title: "Events"
description: "Browse upcoming events, register, and get your ticket"
---

Browse everything your association has scheduled, past and upcoming.

![Member events](/images/member/events.png)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find an event">
    Open an event to see its details — date, location, and any registration requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register">
    Select **Register**. If the event requires payment, you'll be taken through checkout first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your ticket">
    Once registered, open **My registrations** to view or download your ticket — it carries a QR code used for check-in at the event.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Need to cancel? Open your registration and select **Cancel** — subject to whatever cancellation policy your association has set for that event.

You can also add an event to your personal calendar using the `.ics` download on the event page.
