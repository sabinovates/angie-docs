---
title: "Member directory"
description: "Find and connect with other members of your association"
---

The directory lists other members of your association, respecting whatever privacy choices each member made on their own profile.

![Member directory](/images/member/directory.png)

Use the search box to find someone by name. Only the fields a member chose to share publicly appear in their directory entry — see [Profile & privacy](/member-guide/profile) to control your own visibility.
