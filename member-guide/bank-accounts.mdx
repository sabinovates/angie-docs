---
title: "Bank accounts"
description: "Save a bank account for wallet withdrawals"
---

Before you can withdraw from your [wallet](/member-guide/wallet), you need at least one saved bank account.

![Member settings page, where bank accounts are managed](/images/member/bank-account.png)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">Go to **Settings → Bank accounts**.</Step>
  <Step title="Add an account">
    Select your bank, enter the account number, and confirm the account name.
    You'll get an email confirming it was added.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a default">
    If you save more than one account, mark the one you want used by default for
    withdrawals.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Removing an account also sends you a confirmation email, so you notice right away if an account disappears without your doing it.
