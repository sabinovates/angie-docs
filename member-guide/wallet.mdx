---
title: "Wallet"
description: "Deposit, withdraw, and send money to other members"
---

Your wallet is a balance you hold within the association — useful for paying dues without a card on hand, or for sending money directly to another member.

![Member wallet](/images/member/wallet.png)

## Depositing

Select **Deposit**, enter an amount, and complete payment. Your wallet balance updates once payment clears, and you'll get an email receipt.

## Withdrawing

Select **Withdraw** and choose which [saved bank account](/member-guide/bank-accounts) to send it to. A withdrawal request needs at least one bank account on file, and you'll get an email confirming the request was made.

## Sending to another member

Select **Send**, pick the member, and enter an amount. Both you and the recipient get an email confirming the transfer — you as the sender, them as the receiver.

<Warning>
  A wallet-to-wallet transfer can't be reversed from the member portal. Double-check the recipient before sending.
</Warning>
