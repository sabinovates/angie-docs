---
title: "Profile & privacy"
description: "Update your personal details and control who can see them"
---

Your profile holds the personal details your association keeps on file, and it's also what other members see in the [directory](/member-guide/directory).

![Member profile page](/images/member/profile.png)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Profile">
    Select **Profile** in the portal sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your details">
    Update your name, contact information, and other personal fields, then save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your privacy preferences">
    Choose which profile fields (like your phone number or address) are visible to other members in the directory versus kept visible only to admins.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Privacy settings only control what other **members** can see. Association admins can always see your full profile.
</Note>
