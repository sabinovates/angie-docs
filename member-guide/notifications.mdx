---
title: "Notifications"
description: "Your in-portal notification inbox"
---

Notifications tell you about things that need your attention — a dues reminder, an event registration confirmation, a response to a group request — separate from email or SMS.

![Member notifications](/images/member/notifications.png)

- Select a notification to open whatever it's about.
- Mark one notification, or all of them, as read.
- Delete any notification you don't need to keep.

<Tip>
  Which categories of notification you also get by email, SMS, or WhatsApp is controlled from [Settings](/member-guide/settings), not here.
</Tip>
